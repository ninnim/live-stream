# 13 — AI Features

AI is a later capability layer, not a dependency for the basic broadcast path.

## AI Live Assistant

- summarize chat
- detect common questions
- suggest replies
- identify spam/toxicity signals
- surface important messages

## AI Director

Future source selection based on scene context, speaker detection, product visibility, and configurable business rules.

## AI Clips

- detect high-value moments
- create clips automatically
- generate titles/captions
- resize for short-form destinations

## Transcription / translation

- live captions
- post-live transcript
- multilingual subtitle generation
- chat translation

## AI analytics

- identify engagement spikes
- summarize audience sentiment with caution and transparent confidence
- explain drop-off points
- recommend future stream improvements

## Guardrails

- AI should not secretly publish destructive actions.
- Human approval is required for high-impact moderation or publishing until explicitly enabled.
- AI output must be traceable to the session/time range used.
- AI jobs must be asynchronous and retryable.
