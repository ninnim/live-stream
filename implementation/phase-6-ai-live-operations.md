# Phase 6 — AI Live Operations

## Objective

Add AI features that assist rather than destabilize the core broadcast path.

## Scope

- AI live assistant.
- AI moderation support.
- Transcription.
- Translation.
- Automatic highlights.
- Clip generation.
- AI analytics.
- AI director experiments.

## Acceptance criteria

AI features are asynchronous, observable, permissioned, and independently disableable. Core streaming remains functional when AI services are unavailable.
