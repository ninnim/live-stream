# Phase 4 — Multi-Device & Collaboration

## Objective

Allow several devices and team members to participate in one Live Session.

## Scope

- QR/pairing flow.
- Device roles.
- Permission checks.
- Source presence and health.
- Operator controls.
- Moderator role.
- Device revoke.

## Acceptance criteria

Multiple authorized devices can join one session and expose their source/status to the control room without sharing permanent credentials.
